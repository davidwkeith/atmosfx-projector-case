      Scare mode

      A calm clip loops without a gap; on a trigger (the "Scare" switch in your Home app, the motion sensor, DMX, or the button above) one scare clip plays, then it goes back to the calm clip. Turn it on with Settings > Playback > Mode.

        Schedule

        Times are local to the Pi. An off time earlier than the on time means after midnight. The schedule only acts at these times: switching on or off by hand holds until the next one.

        Settings

        Changes here are saved on the Pi and override /etc/default/videofx. "Reset" goes back to the file value, or the built-in default.

        Stops playback for a few seconds; the service comes back by itself and restores the last state.
