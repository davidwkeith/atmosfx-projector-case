      Scare mode

      A calm clip loops without a gap; on a trigger (the "Scare" switch in your Home app, the motion sensor, DMX, or the button above) one scare clip plays, then it goes back to the calm clip. Turn it on with Settings > Playback > Mode.

        Schedule

        Times are local to the Pi. An off time earlier than the on time means after midnight. The schedule only acts at these times: switching on or off by hand holds until the next one.

        DayOnAt sunsetOff

        Quiet hours

        While quiet, the volume is capped (0 mutes) and scares can be off, whoever asks: this page, Home, the schedule, the motion sensor. An end earlier than the start runs past midnight.

        Settings

        Changes here are saved on the Pi and override /etc/default/videofx. "Reset" goes back to the file value, or the built-in default.

        Some changes need a service restart.

        Restart service

        Stops playback for a few seconds; the service comes back by itself and restores the last state.

        Backup

        One file with the settings, schedule, quiet hours, playlist and Matter pairing (not the videos). It contains the pairing keys and the password hash: keep it private.

        Download backup

        Only restore the pairing onto the same Pi, or when the old Pi is gone for good. Two devices with one Matter identity confuse Apple Home and both misbehave. Without it, this Pi keeps its own pairing (pair it again if it's new).
